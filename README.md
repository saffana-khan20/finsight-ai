FinSight AI helps you keep track of personal and shared finances. You can record expenses, salary and other income, set savings goals, and see how your spending affects your monthly budget.

The app can automatically categorize expenses, estimate future spending from past transactions, suggest category budgets, and flag unusual spending. It also offers saving tips, reminders, and monthly PDF reports. For group expenses, you can split costs, upload receipts, leave comments, and track settlements.

FinSight AI is built with Flask, MySQL, and JavaScript. Google Gemini is optional for generating saving tips.
